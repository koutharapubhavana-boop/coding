🎓 Student Result Calculator

Welcome to the Student Result Calculator!

This system helps students calculate their academic performance easily and provides useful suggestions to improve their marks.

📋 Instructions

  • Enter the student's name.
  • Enter marks for all five subjects.
  • Marks should be between 0 and 100.
  • Make sure all marks are entered correctly.
  • Click the Calculate Result button.
  • The system will calculate total marks, average, percentage and grade.
  • The system will also display Pass or Fail.
  • Personalized suggestions will be provided based on your marks.
  • Subjects with lower marks will be shown for improvement.

📚 Tips to Get Good Marks

  • Study regularly instead of studying only before exams.
  • Give extra time to difficult subjects.
  • Practice previous questions and important problems.
  • Revise the topics you study every day.
  • Follow a simple daily study timetable.
  • Take short breaks while studying.
  • Ask your teachers when you do not understand a topic.
  • Try to improve your marks in every examination.
